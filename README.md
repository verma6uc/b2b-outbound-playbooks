# B2B Outbound Playbooks

Proposed outbound sales playbooks for B2B software, SaaS, AI services and IT services companies. Ready to use, free to adapt.

> Maintained by the team at [MachGTM](https://machgtm.com). Timelines and targets below are planning examples to adapt to your market.

## Playbooks

### 1. The New Logo Playbook
**Goal:** Land your first 10 enterprise customers from scratch  
**Best for:** Early-stage SaaS, Series A-B  
**Timeline:** 90 days

#### Phase 1: ICP Definition (Week 1-2)
1. List your 3 best customers — what do they have in common?
   - Industry, size, tech stack, geography
   - The person who bought (title, seniority)
   - The signal that made them ready to buy
2. Define 3 negative ICP signals (who you should NOT pursue)
3. Write your one-sentence value prop: "We help [ICP] achieve [outcome] without [pain]"

Use the `build_icp` tool in [Mach GTM MCP](https://github.com/verma6uc/mach-gtm-mcp) to structure this.

#### Phase 2: Signal-Based Targeting (Week 3-4)
Don't spray and pray. Find accounts showing buying signals:
- Hiring SDRs or BDRs → ask what work the team is adding
- Recent funding → investigate the stated use of funds
- An executive's dated post → inspect the actual request
- A complementary tool → check whether it affects service fit
- Expanding headcount → confirm the relevant project and purchasing route

#### Phase 3: First 50 Prospects (Week 5-6)
Criteria:
- Match ICP (firmographics + technographics)
- At least 1 buying signal
- You can find the right decision-maker's contact

Research each one manually. 50 is small enough. Find:
- 1 personal hook (their post, company announcement, interview)
- 1 supported request or an unanswered question about their work
- 1 proof point from your portfolio that matches

#### Phase 4: Sequence Execution (Week 7-10)
Design a sequence for the account and permitted channels. Set the reply baseline and target from your own observations.

Use [Mach GTM](https://machgtm.com) to automate the research and drafting while keeping human approval.

#### Phase 5: Learn and Iterate (Week 11-12)
- Which signals correlated with replies?
- Which subject lines opened?
- Which personas responded most?
- What objections came up repeatedly?

Update ICP and repeat.

### 2. The Competitive Takeaway Playbook
**Goal:** Win deals from a specific competitor  
**Best for:** Teams with a clear differentiated alternative  
**Timeline:** 30-day sprint

#### Setup
1. Identify 3 specific weaknesses of the competitor your prospects complain about
2. Find customers who switched from competitor to you — get quotes
3. Build a "switching guide" (PDF or landing page)

#### Targeting
Find accounts that:
- Use the competitor (check BuiltWith, G2 reviews, job posts)
- Have recently complained about the competitor on LinkedIn/Reddit/G2
- Have a verified renewal or review date from an authorised source

#### Messaging
Lead with the competitor's weakness, not your product's features.

```
Subject: [Competitor] renewal coming up?

Hi [Name],

Most [title]s we talk to who use [Competitor] mention [specific 
weakness]. Usually shows up as [concrete symptom].

[Your company] was built specifically to solve that. [1 sentence 
on how]. [Customer who switched]: "[quote]".

Happy to do a comparison call before your renewal?
```

### 3. The Inbound Follow-Up Playbook
**Goal:** Convert website visitors, trial signups, and content leads  
**Best for:** Teams with some inbound volume  
**Timeline:** Ongoing

#### Scoring Inbound Leads
High priority (contact within 1 hour):
- Demo requested
- Pricing page visited 3+ times
- Trial started by someone with a matching title

Medium priority (contact within 24 hours):
- Content downloaded
- Blog post visited from specific company
- LinkedIn ad clicked

Low priority (nurture only):
- Newsletter signup
- Single-page visit

#### Inbound-First Outreach
The advantage: they know who you are. Use it.

```
Subject: Re: your interest in [product]

Hi [Name],

Saw you [action they took]. Wanted to reach out directly.

Most [title]s who check us out are dealing with [common pain]. 
Is that what brought you here, or something else?

Happy to walk you through [specific thing relevant to their action] 
in 15 minutes.
```

#### Inbound + Outbound Combo
If someone from a company visited but didn't fill a form — find the decision-maker and reach out:

```
Noticed someone from [Company] looking at [product]. 
Reaching out because it might be relevant — [1-line value prop].
```

### 4. The Partner/Channel Playbook
**Goal:** Generate pipeline through strategic partners  
**Best for:** Teams with complementary tooling in target market  
**Timeline:** 60-90 days

#### Finding Partners
Look for companies that:
- Sell to the same buyer (same ICP)
- Solve a complementary problem (not competing)
- Already have trust with your target market

Examples for [Mach GTM](https://machgtm.com) partners:
- CRMs (HubSpot, Salesforce consultants)
- Sales training firms
- GTM advisory firms
- Revenue operations consultants

#### Partner Activation
1. Build a co-marketing piece together (guide, webinar, template)
2. Create a partner referral landing page
3. Set up a mutual Slack channel for referrals
4. Share a % of closed revenue (10-20% typical)

#### Partner Outreach Template
```
Hi [Name],

I work at [Your company]. We help [ICP] with [what you do].

I think there's a natural fit between what you do and what we do — 
we sell to the same buyers but solve different problems.

Would you be open to a 20-minute call to explore whether a 
referral arrangement makes sense for both of us?
```

### 5. The Case Study Multiplication Playbook
**Goal:** Turn 1 customer win into 10 outbound conversations  
**Best for:** Teams with at least 1-2 strong customer results  
**Timeline:** 1-2 weeks to set up, ongoing

#### Building the Case Study
Don't write a boring PDF. Build a story:
- **Before:** What was the situation? What was the pain?
- **Why us:** Why did they choose you vs. alternatives?
- **The work:** What specifically did you do?
- **The result:** Specific, measurable, attributed

#### The "Same Situation" Approach
Find 50 companies with the same profile as your case study customer.

```
Subject: What [Customer] achieved in 90 days

Hi [Name],

[Customer] — [same industry, same size as prospect] — had 
the exact same problem you're probably dealing with: [pain].

In 90 days: [specific result].

The approach: [1 sentence on how you did it].

[Company] looks like a similar situation. Worth 15 minutes to 
see if the same approach applies?
```

#### Tracking
Use analytics and conversation history your team is authorised to inspect. A company-level visit leaves the individual visitor and purchasing need unresolved. Review the [case-study evidence guide](https://machgtm.com/blog/customer-proof-project-context) before adapting proof for another account.

## Measure the playbook

Use a fixed account group and reporting cutoff. Count substantive replies, meetings booked, meetings held and accepted meetings with their denominators. Include review time and failed work.
